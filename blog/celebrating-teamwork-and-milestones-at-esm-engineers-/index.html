Celebrating Teamwork and Milestones at ESM Engineers

July 22, 2025

This month, ESM Engineers came together for a special evening to celebrate the dedication and hard work that went into three major projects: Kinder Morgan, NRG, and Jesse R. Brown VA.

📸 Smiles all around! A well-deserved dinner to celebrate the hard work and dedication behind three major projects.

Each of these efforts pushed us to grow—technically, professionally, and as a team. From tight timelines to highly technical scopes, our engineers and project staff rose to the challenge with focus, collaboration, and commitment.

A Moment to Pause and Appreciate

While we’re usually heads-down in drawings, deadlines, and coordination calls, the dinner offered a chance to pause and reconnect. Sharing a meal with colleagues—some of whom worked side-by-side daily, others remotely—gave us a moment to reflect on how far we’ve come and to celebrate the people behind the progress.

Looking Back and Moving Forward

The success of these projects is a testament to the strength of our team. It’s the result of late nights, double-checking details, troubleshooting under pressure, and always putting quality first.

We’re incredibly proud of what we’ve accomplished—and even more excited for what’s ahead. Moments like these remind us that every milestone is worth celebrating.

Here’s to the people who make it happen.
